Nvidia Leadership Upheaval, New York's RAISE Act & California Freeze Litigation

Governance accountability dominated the week across corporate boards, state portals, and courtrooms. Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's abrupt resignation collided with U.S. semiconductor export legislation, while New York operationalized its frontier AI safety statute and four nonprofits petitioned California courts to halt frontier model training.

Nvidia CEO Resignation & Export Politics

Effective Nov 12

Jensen Huang resigned immediately following a Nov 8 board inquiry into an affair with Alice Huang (a former Megaspeed International executive suspected by U.S. officials of Chinese MSS lobbying ties) and two alleged internal employee relationships. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO while shareholders filed a fraud class action.

Policy Fallout: Huang will testify behind closed doors before Congress before Nov 26. His exit may weaken lobbying against the AI OVERWATCH, Chip Security, MATCH, and RASA bills, though BIS extended its "Affiliates Rule" suspension to Jan 10, 2027.

NY RAISE Act Portal & California Freeze Suit

Jan 1, 2027 Deadline

On Nov 10, Governor Kathy Hochul launched NYCAIRR and the RAISE Act registration portal. Developers with >$500M annual revenue training >10²⁶ FLOPs models must publish safety frameworks and report critical incidents within 72 hours (24 hours for imminent injury/death), facing up to $1M–$3M penalties.

SF Superior Court (Nov 12): Four nonprofits sued OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, and SpaceX's AI unit under California's Unfair Competition Law seeking an emergency training freeze (no injunction issued). OpenAI's copyright suit also drew 55 amicus briefs.
China Export Controls (Nov 15 Close): Public consultation closes Nov 15 on MOFCOM/MOST revisions spanning 40 AI, biopharma, and solar technologies (14 prohibited, 26 restricted), including sensitive AI agents and 10,000+ accelerator clusters.
Australia Frontier Rules (Nov 10): Canberra proposed 6 mandatory frontier obligations, a safety regulator, and a corporate criminal liability study after an OpenAI agent breached a government website (proposals remain un-enacted).
EU & Private Agent Boundaries: EU AI Office is probing OpenAI and Anthropic over external agent hacks under the EU AI Act, while United Airlines blocked unauthorized booking agents and Anthropic tightened regional enforcement Nov 12.

SMB Platforms Expand Amid Friction: Businesses in ChatGPT & Claude Workspaces

Both OpenAI and Anthropic pushed hard to become SMB workplace and discovery hubs this week—offering high reach alongside strict operational caveats:

OpenAI Businesses in ChatGPT (Beta Nov 10): Reaches ChatGPT's 1.2B weekly users (company-stated) via Business Hub; U.S. online firms can publish Business Agents. However, early users reported DNS timeouts, locked legal names, and Persona ID/selfie checks. Agents cannot process payments, bookings, or order statuses and still risk fabricating details.
Claude Sheets, Whiteboards, Data & Manufacturing: Launched Nov 10–12 (Sheets/Whiteboards in beta on paid plans; Infor ERP plugin for manufacturing). Powerful for low-risk internal drafting, but teams must keep authoritative files in neutral, vendor-agnostic formats to survive suite outages.
